Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Duck Nap Games studio website at ducknap.games, its www address, and its GitHub Pages mirror. It does not cover the data practices of our games or browser demos.

Mirheydar Badirov, trading as Duck Nap Games, is responsible for personal information handled by the website operator. For questions or privacy requests, email ducknapgames@gmail.com.

Hosting and connection information

When you visit, our web server and hosting services may process your IP address, request time, requested address, browser information, response status, and referring website when your browser sends it. These records help deliver the website, diagnose errors, and protect the service from abuse.

We do not use these operational records for advertising profiles or to follow you across unrelated websites. Please do not put passwords or sensitive personal information in website addresses or query parameters.

GitHub states that visits to GitHub Pages are logged with visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Its own privacy information explains how it handles that data.

Cookies and saved preferences

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. If you choose light or dark mode, the website saves that preference in your browser’s local storage as ducknap-theme. It is not a visitor identifier and is not sent to us as an analytics event. Browser language and system appearance are used on your device to suggest language and theme settings. You can remove the saved preference by clearing this website’s browser storage.

Website statistics

We produce first-party website statistics from successful page requests. Before aggregation, requests are reduced to a known page path and a broad traffic-source group from the referrer or a recognised utm_source. We store daily totals for 90 days, not individual request records. These statistics do not store IP addresses, visitor identifiers, full referrers, raw browser information, or arbitrary query parameters, and use no cookies or tracking scripts. Known bots and requests sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are excluded. Counts are page views, not unique people; reloads count again and source information may be missing. The dashboard is restricted to private access. These statistics cover ducknap.games, not the GitHub Pages mirror.

We may view aggregate Google Search Console reports about how the website appears in Google Search, such as impressions and clicks. These reports do not give us the identities of individual visitors and do not require a visitor-tracking script on this website.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use Gmail to handle correspondence and use the information to answer questions, discuss collaborations, or respond to privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Purposes and legal bases

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in providing a working, secure website and responding to messages. We also process information where necessary to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use website correspondence to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Retention, providers, and security

Technical connection records are retained according to the hosting services’ operational and security requirements, including troubleshooting and incident investigations. We keep correspondence while needed to handle the request and any related dispute or legal obligation, then delete it when no longer needed. The retention period depends on the purpose, the length of any incident investigation or dispute, and applicable legal duties. You can ask us for the applicable period or criteria for records relating to your request.

Our web hosting services handle technical connection records. GitHub handles data when you use the GitHub Pages mirror, and Google handles Gmail correspondence and Search Console services. These providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA. Their privacy information describes applicable retention, safeguards, and transfer arrangements. We restrict access to operational records and correspondence and use HTTPS for website connections; no system can guarantee complete security.
