# Language flag artwork

Original SVGs from https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags at revision
`c09927e63705529bbf59ca6684cd9b23225dddad`, matching the game's flag sources.
Upstream collects public-domain flag designs from Wikimedia Commons.

The SVGs are unchanged. Display them with `object-fit: contain` to preserve their
original proportions, symbols and colors. Flags supplement native language names;
they do not imply that a language is exclusive to a country.

English: GB; Japanese: JP; Korean: KR; German: DE; Traditional Chinese: TW;
French: FR; Spanish: ES; Brazilian Portuguese: BR; Italian: IT; Arabic: SA;
Thai: TH; Turkish: TR; Indonesian: ID; Polish: PL; Dutch: NL; Vietnamese: VN.
